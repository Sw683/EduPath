NITI Aayog, Government of India

NITI Aayog Internship Scheme

An internship opportunity providing students exposure to government functioning, policy-related work, analysis and research.

Relevant Fields & Domains:
Public Policy Economics Governance Data & Analytics Social Development Health & Education
Eligibility Summary: Undergraduate students (completed 2nd year/4th semester with ≥85% in Class 12) or postgraduate/research scholars from recognized universities (≥70% in undergraduate degree).
Duration: Minimum 6 weeks, maximum 6 months.
Stipend Status: Unpaid (honorary internship scheme; practical learning experience with official government certificate upon completion).
Current Status: 📅 Monthly Window: Online application opens from the 1st to the 10th of every month only. Check official portal.

Application Workflow

📋 How to Apply

Follow these 7 systematic steps to navigate the official government application process smoothly.

  1. 1

    Open the official portal

    Use only the authentic government URL (e.g. domains ending in .gov.in, .nic.in, or approved statutory bodies like aicte-india.org). Never apply through third-party intermediaries.

  2. 2

    Read the eligibility requirements carefully

    Review degree requirements, minimum aggregate percentage cutoffs (e.g., 60%–85%), academic year criteria, and eligible courses specified in the official scheme brochure.

  3. 3

    Check the application opening and closing dates

    Take note of strict application cycles (e.g., NITI Aayog accepts forms only between the 1st and 10th of each month, while MeitY and MoEFCC run seasonal intake windows).

  4. 4

    Prepare your CV/resume and required academic documents

    Gather scanned marksheets, student ID card, college No-Objection Certificate (NOC) / Bonafide Certificate signed by your Dean/HOD, and a concise statement of purpose (SOP).

  5. 5

    Complete the application on the official portal

    Fill in your personal, educational, and vertical preference details accurately on the government portal. Double-check all uploaded documents for legibility before final submission.

  6. 6

    Save your application/acknowledgement details

    Download the submitted application PDF and record your registration ID, acknowledgement reference number, and date of submission for tracking and correspondence.

  7. 7

    Follow the official portal for selection updates

    Monitor the official ministry/department portal announcements and check your registered email regularly for interview invites, merit list releases, or offer letters.

Before Applying

Protect yourself from fraudulent advertisements and verify every opportunity before sharing personal information:

  • ✓
    Verify the opportunity on the official government website. Confirm that the vacancy is listed directly on a verified .gov.in, .nic.in, or authorized statutory body website.
  • ✓
    Check eligibility carefully. Review minimum aggregate grades, graduation status, required disciplines, and institution recognition before submitting.
  • ✓
    Check the latest deadline. Government application dates can be updated or closed early once quotas are fulfilled. Confirm active dates on the official portal.
  • ✓
    Never pay an unofficial person or website for an application. Official government internship applications in India do NOT require application processing fees, security deposits, or registration charges.
  • ✓
    Do not share passwords, OTPs or sensitive account credentials with anyone. Government departments will never request passwords, banking OTPs, or UPI PINs for an internship application.
  • ✓
    Be careful of fake internship advertisements. Beware of unofficial social media channels, suspicious WhatsApp groups, or third-party agencies claiming guaranteed government placement.
  • ✓
    EduPath does not guarantee selection. Selection is conducted solely by the respective government department, ministry, or participating organisation based on merit.
